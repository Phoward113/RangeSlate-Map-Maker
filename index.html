Saved to your Downloads
  1. Make sure your phone is on the X4's Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap Open X4 page. On Android it opens in Samsung Internet, which can reach the X4. If nothing opens, tap Copy address and paste it into Samsung Internet.
  3. Open the rangecard folder, tap upload, and pick the map from Downloads. The file name must end in .png (for example Back-40.png), or the X4 won't show it.

X4 address

Try 192.168.4.1 first. Upload maps into the rangecard folder on the X4's SD card.

How to send

  1. Make your maps on the Create Map page while you have internet, and tap Keep for Data Card.
  2. On the X4, turn on file transfer and scan its QR code to join the X4's Wi-Fi.
  3. Tap Send to Data Card on a map. It saves the map to your Downloads.
  4. Tap Open X4 page, open the rangecard folder and upload the map.
  5. The file name must end in .png. RangeSlate names maps this way for you; if you rename one, keep the .png at the end.
